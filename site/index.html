An AI back office for a consumer-goods brand, measured on data it never saw.

It reads invoices, matches retailer payments, investigates deductions, forecasts demand and finds lost sales. Every method was written down first, then scored once on unseen public data. Where a model lost, it was not shipped. Everything on this page is live and clickable, built from the real results.
