# Test CPG Intelligence OS in 15 minutes

Open the public copy: **https://akilalours.github.io/cpg-intelligence-os/**
(or run it locally: `make setup && make db && make up`, then http://127.0.0.1:3100).

The public copy is read-only: buttons that record decisions show a message instead of saving.

| # | Task | Where | What a good answer looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Find the store and week that lost the most sales, and the item behind it | Lost sales | A store, a week, an item, and roughly how much was lost |
| 2 | Find a series where LightGBM did worse than simple smoothing. Why might that happen? | Planning | An item, and a reason in your own words |
| 3 | At a 30% margin, which price-cut depth paid back most often in real cuts? | Promotions | A depth range and the share that paid back |
| 4 | Pick one open deduction. Would you dispute it? What evidence decided it? | Cash & deductions, Investigate | Dispute or accept, and the evidence you used |
| 5 | Pick one result on the Proof page and check how it was measured | Proof | Whether you find the method convincing, and why |

**Then file a report:** https://github.com/AKilalours/cpg-intelligence-os/issues/new?template=user-test-report.yml

Reports appear in the README as written. Nothing is edited except removing personal contact details.
